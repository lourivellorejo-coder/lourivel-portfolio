Reconciliation & cleanup

Documented corrections and open-item review

A reconciliation workpaper shows how discrepancies are investigated, corrected when supported, and clearly flagged when client verification or missing documents are still required.

Completed work

Reconciliation and correction logic

  • Multiple bank and credit-card accounts reconciled and documented as completed.
  • Duplicate deposit or fee treatment identified and reclassified to avoid double recording.
  • Beginning-balance difference corrected with a supported journal entry.
  • Book and bank balances reviewed for agreement after correction.
Professional judgment

Unresolved items clearly flagged

  • Unsupported classifications are not forced into the books.
  • Items requiring confirmation are held for verification pending source documentation.
  • Missing statements or documents are specifically listed for follow-up.
  • Open items are documented so the client or reviewer can make the final supported decision.
What this demonstrates

Organized books, traceable corrections, review-ready output.

The goal is not only to post transactions, but to understand the flow, reconcile the evidence, document exceptions, and leave a clear trail for the client, accountant, or reviewer.
